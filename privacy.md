# Privacy Policy — Belante

**Last updated:** May 10, 2026
**App:** Belante — Subscription Tracker

---

## In one sentence

Belante does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

---

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Belante operates entirely on your device.

We do not collect, see, or have access to:
- Your subscription names, amounts, or dates
- Your personal information (email, phone, name)
- Your device information (model, ID, IP address)
- Your usage patterns or analytics
- Your location

## Where your data is stored

All your data — subscriptions, settings, preferences — is stored **locally on your device** using your device's built-in storage (`localStorage` on web, app sandbox storage on mobile).

This data:
- Stays on your device
- Is not synced to any server
- Is not accessible to us or any third party
- Is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data

## What we do not do

- We do not have a server that stores your data
- We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or any tracking
- We do not show advertisements
- We do not sell, rent, or share your data (we have no data to share)
- We do not require an account or login
- We do not connect to your bank or financial accounts

## Backup and Export

Belante allows you to export your data as a JSON file at any time. This export:
- Is generated entirely on your device
- Is saved wherever you choose (your local storage, your cloud account, your email)
- Is never sent to us

When you import a backup, the file is read locally on your device. We never see its contents.

## Permissions

Belante requests the following Android permissions:
- **Storage access (when needed):** Only to read/write backup files when you export or import. Used only at the moment of action.

Belante does **not** request:
- Internet access (no network calls)
- Contacts
- Location
- Camera or microphone
- Phone or SMS

## Children's Privacy

Belante does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for all ages, but as it is a financial tracking tool, we recommend ages 13+.

## Third-Party Services

Belante uses no third-party SDKs, analytics, or services that could collect data.

The only external resource the app loads is the Fraunces font from Google Fonts. Google Fonts may log the font request (this is standard for any website using Google Fonts) but receives no data about you or your subscriptions.

## Changes to this Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update this page with a new "Last updated" date. Since we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly.
